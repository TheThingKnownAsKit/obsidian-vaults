If you transform the value of words into a vectorized space, you can notice that the difference between two vectors can carry semantic meaning. For example, the distance vector between man and woman is similar to the one between aunt and uncle. You have to encode the words on axis of value (for example, gender and age)

This is useful but still needs context to actually generate new words

A <mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">Transformer</mark> adds a concept of attention to a neural network. Every token has some contextual relevance to other tokens, which is modeled with attention. This encodes the contextual meaning we were missing with just vectorized word space
![[Pasted image 20260115102256.png]]

Embedding + position is <mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">pseudo embedding</mark>. This pseudo embedding is used against a query matrix, key matrix, and value matrix. These matrices make up attention

The <mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">queries matrix</mark> is trying to define the questions that should be asked to update the context appropriately. It encodes questions about a word. The questions are represented as number
$$W_{Q}=\begin{bmatrix}
q_{1,1} & \dots & q_{1,n} \\
\dots & q_{i,j} & \dots \\
q_{n,1} & \dots & q_{n,n}
\end{bmatrix}\to Q=x*W_{Q}$$
![[Pasted image 20260115103217.png]]

The <mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">key matrix</mark> weights the map word embedding plus position to answers to the questions in the query matrix
$$W_{K}=\begin{bmatrix}
k_{1,1} & \dots & k_{1,n} \\
\dots & k_{i,j} & \dots \\
k_{n,1} & \dots & k_{n,n}
\end{bmatrix}\to K=x*W_{K}$$
![[Pasted image 20260115103337.png]]

The closeness to token queries and keys overlapping defines <mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">attention</mark>. This is computed as a matrix dot product (vector projection). The keys should align with the queries and the dot product should show this attendance. One query is dot product with a lot of keys and this produces the value
![[Pasted image 20260115103349.png]]

The <mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">value</mark> vector space will be the transformed embedding after dot producting the query and the keys. You multiply the values by the attention to see how specific tokens attend to one another

These all together make up a transformer, which is a way to make extremely human sounding text generation
