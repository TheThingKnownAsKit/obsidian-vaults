*Ethnocentrism* is the tendency to view our culture as superior to other cultures.
"Other focused" orientation
	Our culture is the normal, it's the default setting, etc
	A different culture is the "other" one, the different one

*Intercultural Communication Competence (ICC)* is the ability to communicate effectively and appropriately in various cultural contexts.
	*Motivation*: the root of a person's desire to foster intercultural relationships
		Intrinsic: healthy curiosity and willingness to learn
		Extrinsic: driven by an outside reward like money, power, or recognition
	*Self-/Other Knowledge*: self-/other awareness, mindfulness, and cognitive flexibility
		building self-knowledge: being comfortable with being uncomfortable; be a good listener
		other-knowledge: direct and thoughtful encounters with other cultures
		develop mindfulness and cognitive flexibility (continually supplement and revise existing knowledge to create new categories rather than forcing new knowledge into old categories)
	*Tolerance for uncertainty*: an individual's attitude about and level of comfort in uncertain situations
		develop a higher tolerance for uncertainty (be patient and curious when engaging with others who are different)
