# When Languages Make Contact
When different languages can come into contact, one of three things happen:
**Lingua Franca:** a language used between two interlocutors but is not the first language (or native tongue) of either of the interlocutors.
	French, Chinese, Arabic, Turkish, English
	Can happen because of political, economic, cultural, and technological power as well as colonization and imperialism
**Pidgin / Creole:** A sociolinguistic phenomenon where two people whose first languages are different attempt to communicate using elements from both of their languages in a mixed manner. A **creole** language is a stable natural language developed from pidgin languages. They differ from actual pidgin languages by being nativized by having children speak it as their primary language. Creole languages usually increase in complexity to pidgin languages and become fully functional.

Pidgin languages:
* Use words from both languages
* Mix morphology and syntax
* Use the simplest sounds from both languages
	Obtained language us called pidgin. Characteristics:
* Limited vocabulary
* Simple grammatical rules
* Similar inventory of sounds
* Spoken by a small fraction of the community
* Used for specific purposes such as trade, religion
	Popular pidgin languages:
* Patois (Jamaican & English)
* Basque-Icelandic pidgin (Basque, Germanic & Romance)
* Bearlachas (Gaelic Irish & English)
* Hawaiian (Portuguese, Hawaiian, Cantonese, Spanish, and English)
* Nigerian (English & Nigeria Krio) -> A Lingua Franca throughout Africa
* Portunol (Apanish and Portuguese)
* Denglisch (Deutsch & English)
* Siculish (Sicilian & English)
