*Native Speakerism*
Using things like accents and the way someone speaks to determine if they can teach a language.
	If someone is teaching English but has a non-English speaker accent, they are less likely to be taken seriously, even if they are qualified.

*Instrumental (authorative) power* is power to make someone do something, to enforce authority.
This can be done with language.
	Imperative sentences - giving requests, demands, or advice
	Conditional sentences
	Declarative sentences
*Influential power* is when a person (or group) does not have any authority but is trying to gain power and influence over others
This can be done with language.
	Assertions - (we all know the US is the greatest country in the world), unfounded and untrue, trying to gain power
	Metaphors - the use of established metaphors can be persuasive and evoke the power of memory
