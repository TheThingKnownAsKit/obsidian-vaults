## Why sociolinguistics matters
* Language and society are *interrelated*, and we cannot speak about one without the other
	* language -> social entity
	* society -> a group of people who share the same language
* The *value* of language is based on the society and the people who use it, not on the language itself
	* value judgement on a language -> based on the attitude we hold towards people who use that language
* Language is an act of *identity*
	* essential -> understanding and defining ourselves and others, providing clues about who you are
* Language plays an important role in *understand* social entourage and *the world* around us
	* language -> the ways we think/perceive things (Sapir-Whorf Hypothesis)
