**Language**: a system of sounds, signs, symbols created and accepted by a group of users to express themselves and communicate with others
**Culture**: the total of the inherited and innate ideas, attitudes, beliefs, values, and knowledge that lay shared foundations for social action

Language -> symbolic presentation of a nation/specific community
Language -> symbolic presentation of a culture

**The Sapir-Whorf Hypothesis: Your thoughts, percepts, and actions are influenced or determined by the language you speak.**
Sapir: The 'real world' is to a large extent unconsciously built upon the language habits of the group
Whorf: We dissect nature along lines laid down by our native languages.
There are two versions of the Sapir-Whorf Hypothesis:

**Linguistic determinism**: Language dictates thought. Speaking a certain language makes you unable to think certain thoughts that speakers of other languages can.
**Linguistic relativity**: Language influences (not dictates) thought. Speakers of different languages "end up attending to, partitioning, and remembering their experiences differently simply because they speak different languages". The language we speak shapes what we pay attention to, how we categorize things, and what we remember

Most people think that linguistic determinism is too strong and prefer linguistic relativism. Linguistic relativism is supported by most research.
