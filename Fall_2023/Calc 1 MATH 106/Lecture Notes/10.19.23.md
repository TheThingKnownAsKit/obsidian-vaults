## Related rates
1. Determine if your problem is a related rates problem: are there two quantities that are related to each other and both depend on some other "hidden" variable?
2. Write down a formula relating the two "related quantities". Draw a picture if it's helpful to find the formula.
3. Use the chain rule / implicit differentiation to differentiate your formula in terms of the hidden variable
4. Plug in given values into your derivative formula to solve
