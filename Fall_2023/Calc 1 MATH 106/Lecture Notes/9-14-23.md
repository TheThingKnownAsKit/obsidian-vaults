## Other Trig Functions
**Base Trig Functions**
Tangent: $\frac{\sin(\theta)}{\cos(\theta)}$
Cotangent: $\cos(\theta)\sin(\theta)$
Secant: $\frac{1}{\cos(\theta)}$
Cosecant: $\frac{1}{\sin(\theta)}$

**Derivatives:** 
Sin: $\cos(x)$
Cos: $-\sin(x)$
Tan: $\sec^2(x)$
Cot: $-\csc^2(x)$
Csc: $-\csc(x)\cot(x)$
Sec: $\tan(x)\sec(x)$

## The Chain Rule (fuck)
It helps us find the derivative of composite functions.
*Composite Function Rate of Change = Outside Function Rate of Change * Inside Function Rate of Change*

If g is differentiable at x and f differentiable at g(x), then the composite function C defined by x(x) = f(g(x)) is differentiable at x and :
$C'(x)=f'(g(x))*g'(x)$

It is very very very VERY helfpul to write out what f(x), f'(x), g(x), and g'(x) is before trying to solve it.

Sometimes you have to do it 5 times to solve one problem :). The derivative of g(x) can need a chainrule to solve, and then the derivative of the inner g(x) function can need a chainrule to solve, etc. Pain.
