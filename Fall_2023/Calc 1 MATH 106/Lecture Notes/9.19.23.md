## Derivatives of Inverse Functions
Inverse functions can be useful for solving things such as $\tan(\arctan(\frac{3}{5}))$
$f(f^{-1}(x))=x$

### Inverse Derivative of Exponential
If $f(x)=\sqrt x$ then (f(x))^2 = x
Take the derivative of both sides (using the chain rule on the left)
$2(f(x)) * f^{-1}(x)=1$
Therefore, as expected, $f^{-1}(x)=\frac{1}{2f(x)}=\frac{1}{2\sqrt 2}$

### Inverse Derivative of Log Functions
Derivative of lnx. 
$y=\ln x$
Remember that $f(f^{-1}(x))=x$
$e^{\ln x}=x$
$e^{\ln x} * (\ln x)'=1$
$x*(\ln x)'=1$
$\frac 1x$
^ Derivative of lnx
If it's something like ln(x^2 + 2), then the derivative is 1/function * inside derivative
$\ln(x^2+2)=\frac{1}{x^2+2}*2x$

Derivative of log(x) is $\frac{1}{\ln(10)}*\frac{1}{x}$
Note: The base does not matter here, it'd work even if it wasn't base 10.

### Inverse Derivative of Trig Functions
$\tan(\arctan(x))=x$
$\frac{1}{\cos^2(\arctan(x))}*(\arctan(x))'$
$1+\tan^2(\arctan(x))=\frac{1}{\cos^2(\arctan(x))}$
$\frac{1}{1+x^2}$

Derivative of arctan(x) = $\frac{1}{1+x^2}$
	If it's something inside arctan, it's just the thing squared and the derivative of the thing on the nominator.
Derivative of arcsin(x) = $\frac{1}{\sqrt{1-x^2}}$
