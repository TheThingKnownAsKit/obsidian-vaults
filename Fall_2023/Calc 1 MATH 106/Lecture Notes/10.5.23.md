## Global Extrema
*Global maximum* is the maximum point in the entire graph. f has a global max at p if f(p) is greater than or equal to all the values of f.
*Global minimum* is the minimum point in the entire graph. f has a global max at p if f(p) is less than or equal to all the values of f.
There can actually be MULTIPLE global mins/max points if there's two points at the same y level. (like a parabola if you limit its domain). However, *even if there can be multiple points, there is only ever be one highest/lowest y value.*

The global extrema may not be at inflection points, they can be the end points as well.

#### Extreme Value Theorem
If f is continuous on a closed interval [a, b], then f attains both a global minimum and a global maximum on [a, b].
Basically, global extrema always exist on a closed interval, but may not on an open interval.
