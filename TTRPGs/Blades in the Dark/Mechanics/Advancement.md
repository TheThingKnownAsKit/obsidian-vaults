# PC Advancement
XP is gained in attributes and playbook track, not overall. If unspecified, xp can be put anywhere. If specified, it can only go one place

Gain XP by
- When you make a desperate action roll (including group action), +1 xp for relevant attribute
- At end of session, review xp triggers. +1 xp if it happened at all, +2 if it happened a lot
	- Playbook-specific xp triggers 
	- If you expressed your beliefs, drives, heritage, or background
	- If you struggled with issues from vice or trauma (indulging does not count as struggling unless you overindulge),
- Training during downtime, +1 xp (once per downtime)

Filled xp track = +1 advance. Advance in playbook track means +1 special ability. Advance in attribute means +1 action dot

# Crew Advancement
The crew has a separate XP track

Review end of sessoin xp triggers. +1 if it happened at all, or +2 if it happened a lot or in a major way
- Crew-specifif xp trigger
- Contend with challenges above your current station
- Bolster your crew's reputation or develop a new one
- Express the goals, drives, inner conflict, or essential nature of the crew

When advancement tracker is filled, clear it and take a new special ability OR mark two crew upgrade boxes. Every PC also gets stash equal to crew Tier + 2
