# Tier
Tier is a measure of wealth, influence, and scale. Goes from 0 to 5 (o to V)

- Tier v. Massive gangs. (80 people)
- Tier iv. Huge gangs. (40 people)
- Tier iii. Large gangs. (20 people)
- Tier ii. Medium gangs. (12 people)
- Tier i. Small gangs. (3-6 people)
- Tier 0. 1 or 2 people

Tier is used for:
- Tolling dice when acquiring an asset
- Fortune rolls
- Determining quality level of your items and employed gangs
- Determining scale of employed gangs

# Hold
Hold is how well a faction can maintain their current tier. W indicates weak and S indicates strong

Factions (both yours and others) can lose hold. If they are S they go to W. If they are W they go down a Tier and stays W

**Factions at war temporarily lose 1 hold**. If you are already in weak standing, temporarily go down a Tier (you can go back up a Tier once the war is over)

If your crew is Tier 0 with a weak hold and you lose a hold, your lair comes under threat

# Reputation
Rep is what is sounds like and you need it to advance tiers

You get:
- 2 REP from completing a score
	- If the target is a higher Tier than crew, you get +1 REP per Tier higher
	- If the target is lower, you get -1 REP per Tier lower (min 0)

You need 12 REP to fill the REP tracker on your crew sheet. When filled, you can develop:
- If your hold is weak, it becomes strong. Reset REP to zero
- If your hold is strong, you can pay to increase your crew tier by one. This costs COIN equal to your new Tier x 8. Reset your REP to zero and reduce your hold to weak

REP maxes out at 12, it will not overfill

# Turf
Each piece of turf you claim reduces REP cost to develop by one. Maximum of 6 turf. You keep turf after REP resets

Turf expands hunting grounds

# Status
Status is -3 to +3

- **+3: Allies**. This faction will help you even if it’s not in their best interest to do so. They expect you to do the same for them.
- **+2: Friendly**. This faction will help you if it doesn’t create serious problems for them. They expect you to do the same.
- **+1: Helpful**. This faction will help you if it causes no problems or significant cost for them. They expect the same from you.
- **0: Neutra**l
- **-1: Interfering**. This faction will look for opportunities to cause trouble for you (or profit from your misfortune) as long as it causes no problems or significant cost for them. They expect the same from you.
- **-2: Hostile**. This faction will look for opportunities to hurt you as long as it doesn’t create serious problems for them. They expect you to do the same, and take precautions against you.
- **-3: War**. This faction will go out of its way to hurt you even if it’s not in their best interest to do so. They expect you to do the same, and take precautions against you. When you’re at war with any number of factions, your crew suffers +1 heat from scores, temporarily loses 1 hold, and PCs get only one downtime action rather than two. You can end a war by eliminating your enemy or by negotiating a mutual agreement to establish a new status rating.
