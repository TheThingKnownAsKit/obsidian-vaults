# Freeplay
Do whatever idk. Collect information for the score probably or do roleplay relevant things, be gay, idk <3

When you want to gather information, the GM will ask HOW the character gathers the info OR how they learned it in the past. If it's common knowledge, just tell them. If it's not and there's an obstacle in the way, do an action roll. If it's not common knowledge but there's no obstacle to learning the info, use a fortune role for the quality of info gathered after an action roll
- If time allows (each roll takes time) you can roll multiple times to gather info if one fails
- Action roll has level of effect like normal. Sometimes it might take multiple rolls if you have to navigate somewhere before being able to roll
- If the gather information investigation is too complex to answer with a single gather information roll, start a long-term project to be worked on during downtime
# Engagement Roll
This is the planning phase

All you do is choose what type of plan you are going with, supply the missing detail, pick your item loadout, and do the engagement roll

Types:
- Assault: Do violence to a target. *Detail: The point of attack.*
- Deception: Lure, trick, or manipulate. *Detail: The method of deception.*
- Stealth: Trespass unseen. *Detail: The method of deception.*
- Occult: Engage a supernatural power. *Detail: The arcane method.*
- Social: Negotiate, bargain, or persuade. *Detail: The social connection.*
- Transport: Carry cargo or people through danger. *Detail: The route and means.*

If you don't know the missing detail you can gather information in some ways to discover it 

Notably for loadout, you have access to all of the items on the character sheet. You will decide what your character's load will be that is specifically on hand. Check the box you want to use up to a desired load weight. Italic items don't count towards load.
- 1-3 LOAD: *Light*. You're faster, less conspicuous; you blend in with citizens.
- 4/5 LOAD: *Normal*. You look like a scoundrel, ready for trouble.
- 6 LOAD: *Heavy*. You're slower. You look like an operative on a mission.
- 7-9 LOAD: *Encumbered*. You're overburdened and can't do anything except move very slowly.

THE ENGAGEMENT ROLL BBY
It is a fortune roll starting with 1d for sheer luck. Modify the dice pool according to major advantages or disadvantages
- Is this operation particularly bold or daring? Take +1d. Is this operation overly complex or contingent on many factors? Take -1d.
- Does the plan's detail expose a vulnerability of the target or hit them where they're weakest? Take +1d. Is the target strongest against this approach, or do they have particular defenses or special preparations? Take -1d.
- Can any of your friends or contacts provide aid or insight for this operation? Take +1d. Are any enemies or rivals interfering in the operation? Take -1d.
- Are there any other elements that you want to consider? Maybe a lower-Tier target will give you +1d. Maybe a higher-Tier target will give you -1d. Maybe there's a situation in the district that makes the operation more or less tricky

The engagement roll cuts to the first serious obstacle in their path, not the approach
The engagement roll determines position at this first obstacle. A 1-3 means desperate, 4/5 is risky, 6 is controlled. A crit carries beyond the initial obstacle deeper into the action. Is only relevant for that first starting situation

Linked plans exist when there is a two-pronged approach. Page 131 for more details. Can either be a setup maneuver to improve position for teammates or a sequential need to happen in order
# The Score
Either 1. a criminal activity, 2. seizing a claim, or 3. a special mission (or other but generally one of these three)

PCs set up a new score by:
- choosing a target (from their claims or the faction list)
- By approaching a potential client and asking for work
- By being contacted by an NPC who needs to hire a crew for a job

If you give up a score, you have zero payoff but still HEAT and entanglements

When flashbacks are allowed

Very open ended otherwise. Can be short or long
