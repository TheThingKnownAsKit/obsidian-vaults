A cohort is a gang or expert. Have a type. You can give one an additional type by spending two crew upgrades. On type rolls get dice per quality level, otherwise they get 0 dice

- **Adepts**: Scholars, tinkerers, occultists, and chemists.
- **Rooks**: Con artists, spies, and socialites.
- **Rovers**: Sailors, carriage drivers, and deathlands scavengers.
- **Skulks**: Scouts, infiltrators, and thieves.
- **Thugs**: Killers, brawlers, and roustabouts.

Cohorts have scale and quality equal to crew Tier. Increases when Tier is upgraded

Elite cohorts get +1d when rolling

An expert has a type (area of expertise) and quality equal to current Tier +1. Scale is always 0. Increase in quality as crew advances Tiers

Cohorts must have one or two edges and equal number of flaws
Edges:
- Fearsome: The cohort is terrifying in aspect and reputation.
- Independent: The cohort can be trusted to make good decisions and act on their own initiative in the absence of direct orders.
- Loyal: The cohort can’t be bribed or turned against you.
- Tenacious: The cohort won’t be deterred from a task.
Flaws:
- Principled: The cohort has an ethic or values that it won’t betray.
- Savage: The cohort is excessively violent and cruel.
- Unreliable: The cohort isn’t always available, due to other obligations, stupefaction from their vices, etc.
- Wild: The cohort is drunken, debauched, and loud-mouthed.

PCs can oversee cohort group actions with COMMAND. You can participate with the cohort by also rolling for yourself

Cohorts have four levels of harm:
1. WEAKENED: Reduced effect
2. IMPAIRED: Operates with reduced quality -1d
3. BROKEN: Can't do anything until recovery
4. DEAD: It is destroyed
If amemable, heal all cohorts during downtime. One level by default, two if PC assists with a downtime activity. If destroyed, spend COIN equal to Tier + 2 to restore + two downtime activities
