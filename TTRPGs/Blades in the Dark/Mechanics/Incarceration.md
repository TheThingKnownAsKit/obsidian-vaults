The only way to reduce wanted level is through incarceration. You can frame someone, send a friend, or have a crew member go down. Reduce WANTED LEVEL by 1 and clear all HEAT. Incarceration can happen from investigation and arrest or by turning self in.

Incarceration:
- WANTED LEVEL 0: A few weeks. Or, a beating and suffer level 3 harm with no resistance roll allowed (they would keep going until you're injured :) )
- WANTED LEVEL 1: A month or two
- WANTED LEVEL 2: Several months
- WANTED LEVEL 3: A year or two
- WANTED LEVEL 4: Life or execution

When time is served, make an incarceration roll using your crew's Tier as the dice pool
