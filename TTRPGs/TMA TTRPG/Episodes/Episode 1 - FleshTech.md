## Goals and Themes
The Institute is weirdly well maintained. Reputation is important to them in this universe but it's almost like they don't want people to know what they do even though it is publicly available information

Starts out with all the new characters in the lobby
The assistance takes them to a conference room where Alexander shows up to greet them

- Introduced the rating system that will be central to their investigations and the general flow
	- Step 1: Receive a statement from Tessa
	- Step 2: Investigate
	- Step 3: Write up the incident and record it's rating
		- 0 -> not supernatural
		- 1 -> inconclusive, cannot say either way
		- 2 -> suspicion of supernatural activity
		- 3 -> confirmed supernatural activity
	- (also corresponds to how much xp you get)
- Introduce the Institute's rules
	- Any supernatural activity must be reported
	- Any supernatural artifacts must be submitted to the Institute for processing and security
	- Any supernatural persons must be reported to your supervisor
		- Do not call the police unless a threat of violence is present
	- Archives information is confidential to employees
	- Restricted content requires supervisor permission to access
- Resources they can and cannot use
	- Any equipment deemed suitable by your equipment supervisor
	- Cannot access governmental resources (this is not a government agency)
	- Transportation is provided and costs are covered
	- Basically anything you need to facilitate your investigation can be acquired. Some will require paperwork, some is restricted due to confidential information, but otherwise it's your highest priority to investigate each incident to the fullest
	- The Internet (web1 basically). They are all provided computers and phones (if you're important you get a laptop)

Alexander takes questions before taking them to the archives and introducing them to the players who already work for the Institute
Then introduces them to Tessa who gives them the statement

Statement of Jimmy Young regarding a laptop he purchased six months ago.
Statement begins:
	*Before you say anything, yes, I should have gotten rid of it months ago. I know that. I'm not here for advice, I just need SOMEONE to know what happened before I return it.*
	_
	*I work in one of the plastics factories. The pay is decent, about as much as I can hope I suppose. The fumes alone almost make it not worth it, but I have bills and roommates who would very much so not appreciate me missing my share of the rent. I barely have enough left over to feed myself. I don't mean to throw a pity party, there's ten thousand guys like me within a block or two from where I live, but I need you to understand why I needed it. I'm not crazy, I just. I just need you to understand.*
	_
	*I found it walking home from work one day. I've taken this same route every day for the past five years, but I'd never seen this store before, so it caught my attention. It was pretty standard, all things considered. It was a small hole in the wall with neon signs in the window spelled out "Bargain Bytes" with only a letter or two working. There were so many signs plastered across the windows, hanging from banners, or propped up on boards outside shouting about their unmatchable deals on all electronics and gadgets. "Buy 1 get 1 free!" "80% off flash sale!" "Extra 10% off when you make your purchase past midnight!"*
	_
	*The sheer enthusiasm was enough to make me curious enough to take a look. I figured all it could do was waste a few minutes of my time. The inside was just as tacky as the outside with so many brightly colored signs my eyes struggled to settle on any one thing. They had cardboard cutouts of one of the worst mascot designs I've ever seen. It was this weird little fucker that I think was supposed to be a hard-drive in the shape of a dollar bill. It was wavy, horribly green, and it's eyes were too far apart to even look cute. The flickering lights and grime of the place didn't help the aesthetic either.*
	_
	*While the decor was lacking, the store made good on it's promises. It was selling phones for five dollars, laptops for ten, space heaters for fifty sense. At first, I assumed it was a scam and they were just selling empty cases or some other trick. I picked up on of the laptops and it felt heavy enough. It wasn't in the best condition, a crack or two in the screen and a missing key, but I was starting to get excited. With a laptop, I could maybe find a new job in document processing or something. I found the cashier hidden behind a desk entirely obscured by those cardboard cutouts, purchased it as fast as possible, and quickly went home.*
	_
	*I haven't felt that excited in a long time. I nearly sprinted home because I thought some weird cosmic balance of the universe would have to correct itself and make me get spontaneously hit by a space rock, or something, but the jog home was uneventful. I kind of wish a space rock had hit me, it would have been quicker and simpler I suppose.*
	_
	*Things were fine for a while. To everyone's surprise, the thing actually worked. It had a few quirks but held together for a week or so just fine. The first time something weird happened, I thought it was just another one of it's 'quirks.' I accidentally scraped the case and thought nothing of it until the next time I picked it up, I felt something wet on my hand. *
