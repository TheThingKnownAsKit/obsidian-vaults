
Magic was originally a gift by ancient gods that take the form of great beasts. They haven’t been seen in thousands of years due to the rise of organized civilization. They are mostly passive, but some still choose to gift magic to individuals or interfere with humans (looking at you, death).

## Cores

Humans were basically given magic cores. These have been genetically passed down through the centuries. There used to be specific cores for specific types of magic (like a transfiguration core), but over time all these kind of meshed together to just make one core. Typically located near the heart for optimal production. It starts out as more of a tiny crystal and later turns into a form   

The cores produce magic at a certain rate. This rate can be increased/decreased by different factors. Overuse, underuse, properly paced practicing, magic steroids?(likely in the form of pure magic or diluted?), etc. The core tends to grow with age and use. Like a muscle. Any magic not used will be stored over time. Serious harm can come from using up all the magic/overexerting yourself. If you don’t use enough of it, though, it can lead to becoming an Obscurial. It makes someone incredibly sick and volatile. Incredibly lethal.

The knowledge of these cores is severely primitive. It is both difficult for a human to understand, incredibly subtle, and difficult to even get a core to study. Additionally, depending on how someone dies, cores can dissipate immediately or within a few hours of death.

Little is publicly known about the physical appearance and properties of these cores due to how understudied they are and how quickly they dissipate. They usually take the form of a very light transparent layer over the heart. If someone shows a particular aptitude for one form of magic over the other, an engraved symbol can appear in the core corresponding to the god. Additionally, beasts can select individuals as ‘champions’ or just someone that really embodies their ideals/that they really like. This automatically engraves their symbol on their core, even if a different one is already there. It rarely happens, but someone can also be given more cores if a beast so chooses. It essentially looks like multiple layers on top of each other.

Ancient magic is a specific core in of itself. It is not genetic and incredibly rare. It is also powerful enough to persist after death if stored correctly. It usually corresponds to some kind of prophecy that requires it. It is basically just less restricted magic. The original gifted magic had imposed limits, but ancient magic is more akin to that of the beasts, if not a fraction of its full power. People with ancient magic are typically born with it. It starts out as just an addition to the normal core, but over time, it grows into its own mini heart that produces solely ancient magic. This typically happens around the age of 14 instead of the usual 10 or 1.

These gifts were not exclusive to humans. Many animals were also given cores and thus evolved into the magic animals of today. Wands use the magical properties of these animals in their wands as a sort of conduit to make casting easier, but wands are not required for magic.
