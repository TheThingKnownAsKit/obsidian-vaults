Oldest Draguar sibling, he has largely separated himself from his family (aside from his youngest brother Briar). He owns a cafe in Diagon Alley. His is by far one of the most redeemable members of the family. He is decently kind and reasonable and condemns most of the actions that Franky and his parents take.

After their parents are killed, Ashe is approached by [[Aminah Alanmorr]] requesting permission to adopt Briar. She didn't want to step on any family toes if Briar wanted to stay with their only surviving family. Ashe, being a dickhead, never told Briar that Aminah had asked and became his legal guardian instead. That is, until Briar found out about the dickheadedness and was rightfully pissed. Eventually, Aminah would adopt Briar and he'd move out of Ashe's small apartment above the cafe.
