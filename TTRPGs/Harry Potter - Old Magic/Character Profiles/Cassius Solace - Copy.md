The eldest of the Solace children, he left home shortly after basically being silently disowned by his parents following a [[Metahumans|Werewolf]] attack when he was 13.

He left to live with his estranged aunt for a while before becoming a relic hunter for Aminah Alanmorr. Now, 10 years later, he's decided to come back into his younger siblings' lives following the werewolf attack on Alexandria in [[Year 6]]. He also attends Vivian's funeral in [[Year 7]].
