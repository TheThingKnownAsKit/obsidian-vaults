Anastasia Amos was muggle-born student attending Hogwarts in the late 1800s during the Goblin Rebellions. She was one of two [[Ancient Magic]] wielders alive during this time, and none have been seen since. The other Ancient Magic user being her future wife.

During [[Lenora Adaleen|Ranrok's General]]'s rampage that nearly wiped out the wizarding population in the United Kingdom, Ana was briefly a part of the wizarding Resistance force. However, when she was later betrayed, she became more of an independent force focused solely on defeating the General.

When Ana and the General finally met in battle, there was actually very little fighting and a lot more crying. The two had been very close good friends and maybe roommates in Hogwarts. The exact details of their life together are unknown to historians, but it is believed they went into hiding together and were not seen again.

# Actual Story
Ana wanted her girlfriend back and then they went to live in a cottage together in the woods. Eventually, when their time was up, they did what every Ancient Magic wielder does and died into a painting together. This painting now hangs in the relic hall of [[Aminah Alanmorr]].
