Sepehr Alanmorr was a devout follower of Grindelwald's anti-muggle philosophy. However, unable to do anything about the wizarding government's decision to hide, he took matters into his own hands. He founded [[Alanmorr]] in a pocket dimension as a magic user exclusive city.

Being a raging racist and just about every other ism, ist, and phobia, the city was nothing but corruption and governmental abuse.

Later, Sepehr died mysteriously in his sleep just a few months after his disgraced and "banished" daughter returned from studying abroad.
