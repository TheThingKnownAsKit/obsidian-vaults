He basically goes around peddling medical miracles to a predominantly non-magic demographic, though he'll sell to whoever will buy. He uses petty wizarding tricks to make it seem like it's having an affect, but really he just turned your tongue blue and that's not any more impressive than a sucker. 

He also has a nasty habit of having kids he never sticks around to raise, except one notable exception with Dakota and Jaylin. He kept Dakota for a few years before an accident resulted in permanent adverse health affects (more details in [[Dakota Jones]]). He then gave Dakota back to his mother [[May Jones]]. He kept Jaylin because she had a very pitiable face as a child and people tended to buy more things from him when he presented himself as a wholesome father-daughter business just trying to get by. She stayed with him until late teenage years when he left her in the haste to evacuate a town that was trying to have him arrested. He never came back.
