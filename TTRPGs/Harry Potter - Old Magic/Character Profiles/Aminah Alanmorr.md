Being the daughter of a racist madman, Aminah grew up especially familiar with the more corrupt side of wealth and politics. She was groomed to be just as corrupt as her father, and was for quite a while before being effectively banished for half-breed sympathy. She traveled and studied abroad before returning home just in time for the untimely death of her father.

Aminah assumed control of [[Alanmorr]] and spent the next few decades evading countless assassination attempts and attempting to improve the corruption in the city. She slowly replaced every government official her father had appointed with less racist ones, etc.

Her childhood and rough transition to power left her with a mountain of paranoia taller than Mount Everest. She has traded a lot of her morals for a greater sense of security, and this has also lead to the suffering of Alanmorr residents. Aminah frequently involves herself in less than legal activities to maintain her iron grip on the city and its people. 

In her never-ending quest for power for a sense of security, she has amassed a large and impressive collection of cursed and magical objects. In particular, she is obsessed with obtaining [[Ancient Magic]] and has such acquired [[Lenora and Anastasia's Painting]].

In the Draguar's endless attempts at leveraging more political power, [[Briar Draguar]] was sent to meet with Aminah on several occasions. Seeing herself in him, she quickly grew attached and basically adopted the child. He spent more time at her manor than at home. Later, when Briar's parents are killed, she actually does adopt him.

She married (not legally, in practice) her personal body guard, though someone better tell him that.
