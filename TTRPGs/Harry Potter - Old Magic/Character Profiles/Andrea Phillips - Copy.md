Andy has been the DADA teacher at Hogwarts for a few years prior to the start of the campaign. They tend to be a student favorite with her relaxed and fun demeanor. She would sooner pat you on the back for winning than make a fuss about things like "laws" and "safety". In many ways, she is not super responsible with children. 

However, they were one of the few people who passed the rigorous screening process for Hogwarts professors (a new security measure enforced by Minerva McGonagall, the current headmaster). On top of that, being a (mostly) retired relic hunter made them vastly qualified to teach DADA. They have more experience with the magical dangers of the world than even the most decorated auras. 

She prefers a more artificer style of combat but is still extremely proficient in casting and melee combat, both armed and unarmed. 


Despite her likeable personality and spotless record, Andy is a teacher at Hogwarts for one reason and one reason only: to find a child that they can mold into one that fits the [[The Cult|The Prophecy]]. They are a devout follower of [[The Cult]] and one of the highest ranking members, practically the right hand man of the leader, [[Nicolas Dean]].
