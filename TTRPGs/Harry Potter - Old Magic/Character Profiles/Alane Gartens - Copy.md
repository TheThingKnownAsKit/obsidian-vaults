A student in the grade below the party. Their parents sacrificed their lives for [[The Cult]] and they grew up around that violence. They tend to be a bit conspiratorial and paranoid, but are extremely anti everything to do with the cult.

They work for the Hogwarts' school paper.

Alane has an extreme phobia of magic but cannot get out of attending a magical school. Due to the built up magic, they develop a parasitic Obscurus and become an [[Metahumans|Obscurial]] in later years.
