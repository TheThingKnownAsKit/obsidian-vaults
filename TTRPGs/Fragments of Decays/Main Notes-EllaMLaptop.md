Main villains:
1. Decay (passive)
2. The Nowhere King
	1. Manifestation of the destroyed afterlife. Summoned by Decay. Already destroyed every other continent.
	2. A very very very long time ago, a king went to war to united all the continents. Then, they went to war with the Afterlife. Anything can die if you try hard enough. The Afterlife was struck down after a very very long struggle and all that's left is the Nowhere King. Now, instead of souls going to the afterlife, they get reincarnated over and over again. The endless recycling is wearing on the souls. People are weaker, people are dying, decay is setting in.
	3. The king who led the war against Afterlife didn't know what to do with himself after winning. He withered away on his throne, eventually becoming Decay.
	4. Now, with Decay on the brink of winning, the Nowhere King has come out of dormancy to finally push live over the finish line. The soul damage is too great, they want everyone dead for a chance of combining them into fewer good souls and reinstating the afterlife.
