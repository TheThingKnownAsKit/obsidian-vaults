The Machine is the newest entity in the process of becoming distinct. With the rise of automation recently and the horrendous conditions most of the world is in, people are afraid of becoming replaced or powerless. The Machine is the fear of being another cog in the machine, of being replaced by steel and gears, of the incomprehensible systems that are so much better than you, of wires and rust overtaking all organic things. It is the Iron Rot that will infiltrate humanity and slowly kill it from the inside out. The Wheel Will Turn and it will either run you over or take you with it, but not in the way you want to go. It will crush you into the form it needs and drag you to your destination.

The Machine is known to corrupt other entities as well in a unique way. It can combine with other entities artifacts, avatars, and fears to incorporate them into the assembly.

It basically ate The Extinction. No one knows what climate change is so they can't be afraid of that, but they can be afraid of the consequences they experience.

Emphasis on the facelessness of the puppeteering. You are a cog in the MACHINE, not a puppet of Mrs. Web. Separates it from the web. There's so many parts to the machine you can't even find which one to blame;
