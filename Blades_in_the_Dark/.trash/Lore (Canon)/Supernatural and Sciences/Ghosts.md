*A disembodied spirit, craving vengeance. A rogue spirit is a scourge upon [[Duskwall]], hunted by [[Spirit Wardens]] and sought out as a servant by Whispers and cultists.*


---


Ghosts have to possess living people to consume their spirit energy.


Ghosts are a concentration of [[Electroplasm|electroplasmic]] vapor that resembles their living body and clothes before death. They may weakly interact with the physical world and vice versa. Ghosts can move about by floating and may fly swiftly without tiring. They can slowly flow through small openings as a vapor. Ghosts chill the area around them.


Abilities you can get as ghost traits that ghosts can have but ghosts don't usually have all of them at once:
- Ghosts can disperse their vapor body in order to pass through solid objects for a moment.
- Ghosts can follow the electroplasmic pathways of the [[ghost field]] in order to instantly travel to any place they knew intimately in life, OR to answer the summoning call of a COMPEL.
- Ghosts can momentarily expend energy to strongly interact with the physical world for a few moments (as if they had a normal body). This can be further extended with great effort to include telekinetic force and electroplasmic discharges.
- Ghosts can ATTUNE to the [[ghost field]] in order to take control of a living body. If control is challenged, the ghost must re-attune (risking electroplasmic harm) or leave the body. Control is challenged when 1. they consume spirit energy from the host 2. arcane powers act against you 3. the host's will is driven to desperation. Ghosts can easily and indefinitely possess a [[hull]] or [[hollow]] that has been ritually prepared for them.


Ghosts are extremely vulnerable to arcane powers and [[Electroplasm|electroplasmic]] effects.
