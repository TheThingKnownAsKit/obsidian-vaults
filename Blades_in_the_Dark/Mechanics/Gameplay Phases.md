# Freeplay
Do whatever idk. Collect information for the score probably or do roleplay relevant things, be gay, idk <3

When you want to gather information, the GM will ask HOW the character gathers the info OR how they learned it in the past. If it's common knowledge, just tell them. If it's not and there's an obstacle in the way, do an action roll. If it's not common knowledge but there's no obstacle to learning the info, use a fortune role for the quality of info gathered after an action roll
- If time allows (each roll takes time) you can roll multiple times to gather info if one fails
- Action roll has level of effect like normal. Sometimes it might take multiple rolls if you have to navigate somewhere before being able to roll
- If the gather information investigation is too complex to answer with a single gather information roll, start a long-term project to be worked on during downtime
# Engagement Roll
This is the planning phase

All you do is choose what type of plan you are going with, supply the missing detail, pick your item loadout, and do the engagement roll

Types:
- Assault: Do violence to a target. *Detail: The point of attack.*
- Deception: Lure, trick, or manipulate. *Detail: The method of deception.*
- Stealth: Trespass unseen. *Detail: The method of deception.*
- Occult: Engage a supernatural power. *Detail: The arcane method.*
- Social: Negotiate, bargain, or persuade. *Detail: The social connection.*
- Transport: Carry cargo or people through danger. *Detail: The route and means.*

If you don't know the missing detail you can gather information in some ways to discover it 

Notably for loadout, you have access to all of the items on the character sheet. You will decide what your character's load will be that is specifically on hand. Check the box you want to use up to a desired load weight. Italic items don't count towards load.
- 1-3 LOAD: *Light*. You're faster, less conspicuous; you blend in with citizens.
- 4/5 LOAD: *Normal*. You look like a scoundrel, ready for trouble.
- 6 LOAD: *Heavy*. You're slower. You look like an operative on a mission.
- 7-9 LOAD: *Encumbered*. You're overburdened and can't do anything except move very slowly.

THE ENGAGEMENT ROLL BBY
It is a fortune roll starting with 1d for sheer luck. Modify the dice pool according to major advantages or disadvantages
- Is this operation particularly bold or daring? Take +1d. Is this operation overly complex or contingent on many factors? Take -1d.
- Does the plan's detail expose a vulnerability of the target or hit them where they're weakest? Take +1d. Is the target strongest against this approach, or do they have particular defenses or special preparations? Take -1d.
- Can any of your friends or contacts provide aid or insight for this operation? Take +1d. Are any enemies or rivals interfering in the operation? Take -1d.
- Are there any other elements that you want to consider? Maybe a lower-Tier target will give you +1d. Maybe a higher-Tier target will give you -1d. Maybe there's a situation in the district that makes the operation more or less tricky

The engagement roll cuts to the first serious obstacle in their path, not the approach
The engagement roll determines position at this first obstacle. A 1-3 means desperate, 4/5 is risky, 6 is controlled. A crit carries beyond the initial obstacle deeper into the action. Is only relevant for that first starting situation

Linked plans exist when there is a two-pronged approach. Page 131 for more details. Can either be a setup maneuver to improve position for teammates or a sequential need to happen in order
# The Score
Either 1. a criminal activity, 2. seizing a claim, or 3. a special mission (or other but generally one of these three)

PCs set up a new score by:
- choosing a target (from their claims or the faction list)
- By approaching a potential client and asking for work
- By being contacted by an NPC who needs to hire a crew for a job

If you give up a score, you have zero payoff but still HEAT and entanglements

When flashbacks are allowed

Very open ended otherwise. Can be short or long

# Downtime
Resolve these in order
1. Payoff
2. Heat
3. Entanglements
4. Downtime activities 


**Payoff** is for REP and COIN. Mark on sheets immediately
- Earn 2 REP per score +/- 1 for Tier higher/lower (min zero). If the operation is completely quiet and no one knows about it +0 REP
- Earn COIN based on the nature of the operation and/or any loot they seize. Mark on crew sheet or divvy it up
	- 2 COIN: A minor job; several full purses
	- 4 COIN: A small job; a strongbox
	- 6 COIN: A standard score; decent loot
	- 8 COIN: A big score; serious loot
	- 10+ COIN: A major score; impressive loot
- If there is a crime boss to be paid, subtract COIN equal to Tier + 1. Otherwise, start clock. Every time it fills up, lose 1 status


Take **HEAT** according to the nature of the operation and a few more modifiers. Mark on crew sheet immediately. If the whole track is filled, mark a WANTED LEVEL and clear all HEAT. Excess HEAT rolls over
- Nature of operation:
	- 0 HEAT: Smooth & quiet; low exposure
	- 2 HEAT: Contained; standard exposure
	- 4 HEAT: Loud and chaotic; high exposure
	- 6 HEAT: Wild; devastating exposure
- Other
	- +1 HEAT for a high-profile or well-connected target
	- +1 HEAT if the situation happened on hostile turf
	- +1 HEAT if you're at war with another faction
	- +2 HEAT if killing was involved (whether the crew did it or not)

Note that WANTED LEVEL contributes to law enforcement response and the severity of entanglements


**Entanglements** are determined via table, dice roll, and heat. Roll a number of dice equal to their WANTED LEVEL and use the best result. If the WL is zero, roll two and keep the lowest. Entanglement can be a surprise or in the open. Entanglement's can happen immediately or hold off until later (still in the downtime phase). Crew has to deal with them, describe the full consequence and do not allow intervention

|     | HEAT 0-3                           | HEAT 4/5                    | Heat 6+                         |
| --- | ---------------------------------- | --------------------------- | ------------------------------- |
| 1-3 | Gang Trouble or The Usual Suspects | Gang Trouble or Questioning | Flipped or Interrogation        |
| 4/5 | Rivals or Unquiet Dead             | Reprisals or Unquiet Dead   | Demonic Notice or Show of Force |
| 6   | Cooperation                        | Show of Force               | Arrest                          |
Specified in detail on pg 151. These are specific events


PCs get two **downtime activities** (only one during war). Activities can be chosen more than once. Each additional activity costs 1 COIN or REP
	NOTABLY, only activities specifically on the downtime activities list are limited. You can still do roleplay things like go talk to people, gather information, make action rolls, etc
Activities are: acquire asset, long-term project, recover, reduce heat, train, and indulge vice

Acquire asset is gain temporary use of an asset listed below. Temporary usually refers to the duration of one score but varies (should be a significant period of usage that makes sense). An asset can be put in "standby" for the future (still with a timer I think? It's ambiguous)
- One special item or set of common items (enough for a gang of your Tier scale)
	- If you acquire alchemicals, poisons, bombs, or dangerous gadgets +2 HEAT
- A cohort
- A vehicle
- A service (transportation, use of a warehouse, legal representation, etc)
To acquire, roll the crew's Tier. The result + base quality is the quality you get. Optionally, the GM can set a min quality level that HAS to be achieved to acquire an asset. If you acquire the same asset again, gain +1d to the roll (you can effectively rent an asset indefinitely)
- 1-3: Tier - 1
- 4/5: Tier
- 6: Tier + 1
- Crit: Tier + 2
A long-term project can be set up for permanent acquisition

For a long-term project, describe what you do to advance it, do an action roll, and mark accordingly. 1-3 is one segment, 4/5 is two, 6 is three, a crit is five

To recover, if you have a contact or fellow PC who can provide treatment, roll and mark the healing clock. A PC will use TINKER for dice pool, an NPC will use quality level. 1-3 one segment, 4/5 two, 6 is three, crit is five. When the clock is filled, reduce each instance of harm on your sheet by one level and clear the clock
- Overfill rolls over
- You may heal yourself if you have the PHYSICKER special ability but take 2 stress 
- If you want to "tough it out", take 1 stress and roll 0d
- If you do not know any NPCs or PCs, use acquire asset to gain access to a healer (they can provide service for the whole crew)

To reduce heat, someone just describes what they do and does an action roll. Standard success array

To train, mark 1 xp on any xp track. You get +1 extra xp if you have the training upgrade for 2 total

Indulge vice to relieve stress. Go to your specific purveyor of vice (real things you have to pick, see page 299 for full list). Roll to see how much stress is relieved. Use your worst quality (INSIGHT, PROWESS, or RESOLVE), make an attribute roll, and clear stress equal to the highest die result
- You can choose to let your character be "lost in their vice" aka they indulge off camera while someone plays their alternate PC
- If you clear more stress levels than you had marked, you over indulge. You do something stupid while acquiring/under its influence. PC selects one from the list:
	- ATTRACT TROUBLE: Select or roll an additional entanglement
	- BRAG about your exploits +2 HEAT
	- LOST: Your character vanishes for a few weeks. Play a different one until they return. They heal all harm when returned
	- TAPPED: Your current purveyor cuts you off. Find a new source for your vice
- If you ignore or do not indulge your vice, take stress equal to your TRAUMA


NPC AND FACTIONS ALSO GET DOWNTIME. GM advances their project clocks and chooses a downtime maneuver or two for each faction that they're interested in
- Seize a claim or increase hold, make an enemy vulnerable, or reduce the hold of a vulnerable enemy
- Gather information on the PCs (may be opposed by PC roll) or another subject
- Achieve a short-term goal they're in position to accomplish
- Acquire a new asset
- Call in a favor from another faction
- Employ political pressure or threats to force someone's hand
Basically just pick a few and advance clocks. You can give an ignored faction several downtime's at once to catch them up if needed
