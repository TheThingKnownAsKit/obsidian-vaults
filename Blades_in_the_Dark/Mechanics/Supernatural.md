Encountering a spirit or demon will cause a person to be auto paralyzed with fear or panic them into fleeing. You can resist this with a RESOLVE resistance roll to ignore it. If a character has a lot of experience with the supernatural, such as Whispers, Rail Jacks, and occultists, only strong entities will scare them. Terrible horrors may have additional affects

Being possessed inflicts a level of TRAUMA at the end of every week. Once it suffers the fourth level of TRAUMA, the spirit must either bond with the corpse (becoming a vampire) or abandon it and seek out a new host
