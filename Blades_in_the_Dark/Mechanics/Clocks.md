**Danger Clocks** represent a rising danger (suspicion, a chase/pursuit, alert level, etc)
**Racing Clocks** represent two competing completion bars (for example, PCs can have escape and Bluecoats can have locate)
**Linked Clocks** represent things that need to be done sequentially. One clock unlocks another
**Mission Clocks** are time-sensitive/a window of opportunity. If this clock is filled before another objective is met, plans have to change
**Tug-Of-War Clocks** is one that can be filled and emptied by events
**Long-Term Project Clock** are mostly for downtime activities
**Faction Clocks** are long-term goal clocks for each faction to progress what other factions are up to. Can also be directly impacted by PCs (aid or interfere with)
