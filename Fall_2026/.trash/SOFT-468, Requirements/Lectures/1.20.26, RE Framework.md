<mark style="background: #BBFABBA6;">The goal of requirements engineering is to get the RE cube into the biggest state possible</mark>. The gist is you start with very low values and a very small cube, and as the project goes you expand all 3 dimensions of the cube and end up with a big cube
![[Pasted image 20260120132035.png]]
- <mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">Content</mark> is understanding the requirements and the context
- <mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">Documenting</mark>/specifying is exactly what it sounds like
- <mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">Agreement</mark> is the level of agreement achieved between the stakeholders about the requirements and the context, including detecting and resolving conflicts

This is the foundation for the framework



![[Pasted image 20260120132550.png]]
- The top is out of your control. The core activities and requirements artefacts are in your control
- Iterative process so not really a stage based workflow but a defined process with many back and forths
