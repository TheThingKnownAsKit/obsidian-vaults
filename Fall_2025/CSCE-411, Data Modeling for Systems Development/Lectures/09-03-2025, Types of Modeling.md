<mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">Conceptual Model</mark>: Is designed for clear communication between external roles. On a purely conceptual level, how can we explain this? What are the different kinds of entities, attributes, relationships, and rules relevant? <mark style="background: #BBFABBA6;">This is basically just defining the requirements and the problem</mark>
![[Pasted image 20250903130136.png]]

<mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">Logical Model:</mark> is when you make abstract data structures and operations supporting the conceptual model. Very roughly, how are we going to do this? Typically when diagramming happens to show the abstract structure it is going to take without specify the concrete details. This is also when dependencies and keys are decidedg

<mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">Physical Model:</mark> is when you specify the DBMS (database management system) and things like how it's going to be indexed, the data types, relational tables, etc. This is when you actually design the concrete structure of the database
