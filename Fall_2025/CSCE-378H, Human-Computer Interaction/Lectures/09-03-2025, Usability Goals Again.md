<mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">Nielsen's Goals</mark> for usability are:
1. Learnability
2. Efficiency
3. Memorability
4. Error Prevention/Safety
5. Satisfaction

<mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">Interaction Design Book (ID)</mark> usability goals are:
1. Effectiveness
2. Efficiency
3. Safety
4. Utility
5. Learnability
6. Memorability
7. Satisfaction

<mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">Learnability</mark> is how easy it is for new users to get started and learn how to use the app by themselves. Important for novice users

<mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">Efficiency</mark> is productivity once the system is learned. How many steps does it take to complete a task? How are you trying to reduce effort the user has to put in? Relevant for intermediate and expert users

<mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">Memorability</mark> is how easy a product is to remember after you learn it

<mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">Errors/Safety</mark> is how much are you protecting the user from dangerous conditions and undesirable situations and from perceived fear. Are there ways to easily recover from errors?

<mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">Satisfaction</mark> is the subjective quality of how a system feels. Do you get your little happy brain chemicals after completing a task in the system? Does it hurt?
