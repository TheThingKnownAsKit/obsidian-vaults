<mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">HCI is the study of how people interact with technology</mark>. It is composed of three parts: user, computer, and interface. The goal is to make systems that are useful, usable, and enjoyable to the end user

<mark style="background: #BBFABBA6;">User centered design (UCD) </mark>is the process that puts the users at the center of development at every stage. You iterate and use user feedback to make something that is <mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">first and foremost useful, usable, and enjoyable to the end user</mark>
