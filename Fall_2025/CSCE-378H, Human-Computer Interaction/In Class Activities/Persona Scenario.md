Persona/scenario: Single mother of 3 young children (4, 7, and 9) trying to use a ticket kiosk to get into a play that her adult niece is in

Persona:  
Alex is a middle aged single mother of 3 young children ages 4, 7, and 9. After a long week of working her job in IT customer support, she decided to spend her Friday afternoon attending a play that her adult nephew is the lead actor for. She relies heavily on her regular babysitter to manage work and her kids. The most important thing to her is her family. She is consistently annoyed by the constant apps she has to download for everything and prefers to call instead of booking online whenever possible.

Scenario:  
On a Friday afternoon, Alex parks down the street from the theater and gets her kids out of the car. Usually, she would have her babysitter handle the kids while she attends the play to support her nephew, however, her usual babysitter was unavailable today and she doesn't trust strangers with her children. The family make their way to the theater building and Alex realizes that due to consistent webpage crashes all week and her having to work overtime in customer support to compensate, she forgot to buy the tickets beforehand. Her adult nephew is the lead actor for this play and it is extremely important to Alex that she is there to support him. While struggling to keep her three young kids, aged 4, 7, and 9, from wandering off and panicking from potentially missing her nephew's performance, she notices the ticket kiosk and attempts to operate it. The kiosk has a large touchscreen with an intuitive and efficient design and it only requires a few menu selections and filling out basic information to receive her tickets. This efficient and easy to follow design makes it easy for Alex even while managing three small children. She scans a QR code to add the tickets to her apple wallet and continues on to the play.
