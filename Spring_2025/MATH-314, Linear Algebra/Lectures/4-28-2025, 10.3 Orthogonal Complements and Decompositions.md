## Orthogonal Complements
If W is a subspace of $\mathbb{R}^n$, define the <mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">orthogonal complement</mark> $W^\perp$ of W
$W^\perp=\left\{\overrightarrow{w}^\perp \in \mathbb{R}^n:\overrightarrow{w}^\perp*\overrightarrow{w}=0\text{ for all }\overrightarrow{w}\in W\right\}$
