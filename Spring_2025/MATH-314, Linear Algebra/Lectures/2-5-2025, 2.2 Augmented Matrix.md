## Row-Echelon Form (REF)
The first nonzero entry in a row of a matrix is called the <mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">leading entry or pivot</mark>. When the leading entry is a 1, it is just a leading 1

A matrix is said to be in <mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">row-echelon form (REF)</mark> if:
1. All entries below each leading entry are 0
2. Each leading entry is in a column to the right of the leading entries in the rows above it
3. All rows of zeros, if there are any, are located below nonzero rows
It kinda makes all the nonzero entries crowd the top right corner. Looks like a staircase kinda
REF matrix's are useful because you can back substitute really easy. The bottommost row should be solved or almost solved
![[Pasted image 20250205105820.png]]

Note: The REF of a matrix usually doesn't have a set unique solution. It depends on what elementary row operations you choose to use
## Reduced Row-Echelon Form (RREF)
A matrix is said to be in <mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">reduced row-echelon form (RREF)</mark>  if:
1. It is in REF
2. Each leading entry is 1
3. All entries above and below leading 1's are 0
Looks like a staircase kinda
![[Pasted image 20250205105939.png]]
RREF's are unique solutions

When using an augmented matrix M=[A|b] so solve a SLE, <mark style="background: #BBFABBA6;">our goal is to row reduce M to REF or RREF</mark> (preferably RREF)

## Solving SLEs
The variables, x, y, and z are called<mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">leading variables</mark> because their corresponding columns in the RREF of the augmented matrix have <mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">leading 1's</mark>

Variables that do not have leading 1's in their corresponding column (like w in the previous example) are called <mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">free variables</mark>

For free variables, we always assign them a parameter t or parameters t1,...tk if there are k free variables

In general, if the RREF of an augmented matrix has a row of the form $[0\dots0\ 1\ 1]$ then <mark style="background: #BBFABBA6;">the corresponding SLE has the equation 0 = 1 and the system is inconsistent</mark>
