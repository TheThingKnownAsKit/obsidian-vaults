Types of businesses:
- <mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">Sole proprietorship</mark>
	- Easy to form. Less regulation. No corporate income tax
	- Limited life. Unlimited liability. Difficult to raise capital
	- Use a schedule C to report profit from a sole proprietorship
- <mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">Partnership</mark>
	- About the same as above but with multiple people
- <mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">Corporation</mark>
	- Unlimited life. Easy transfer of ownership. Limited liability. Ease of raising capital
	- Double taxation (except for S-Corp)
	- Cost of set-up and report filing

Agency problems within corporations:
- <mark style="background: #BBFABBA6;">Stockholder-manager conflicts</mark>
	- Shareholders do not want to pay for something the manager wants
	- Shareholders want someone fired and the manager doesn't want to
- <mark style="background: #BBFABBA6;">Stockholder-debtholder conflicts</mark>
	- Debtholders are guaranteed a modest return and stockholders get what is left over
	- Therefore they're incentivized to roll the dice and take excessive risks
