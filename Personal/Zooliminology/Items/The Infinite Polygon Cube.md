The object dubbed "The Infinite Polygon Cube" is a cuboid item originally located in the Far Plane that possesses qualities similar to that of a computer-generated model with an unfathomable amount of polygons. It takes the appearance of a supremely orange cube.

Due to its qualities, the object has the following effects on reality:
- Anyone who looks at it will have their mind overwhelmed and they will have difficulty going through with basic functions.
- Their movements will become choppy and erratic.
- They will have trouble speaking.
- All devices that attempt to record it will crash. This includes via photo, video, or 3D scan. 
- Local time around the cube is broken up into sequential bursts with "lag" in between.
- Upon physical contact, a human will become unconscious. 

The cube is immune to all projectiles. Any projectiles thrown or shot towards it will freeze in place approximately one foot away from the cube and remain there until removed. 

The cube can be successfully contained in a simple room or container and can be handled via tongs or other instruments that allow handling of it from a distance. Handling it for any reason other than research is not recommended.

Analog technology tends to not succumb to the cube's effects, but it still has its own issues. For example, photos taken with a Polaroid camera will not develop the location of the cube in its image. This will occur with even fresh film that otherwise has no issues whatsoever.
