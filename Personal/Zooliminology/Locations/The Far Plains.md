> [!Info] General
> 
> **Size:** Expansive
> **Notable Features:** Comprised of entirely exteriors. Features expansive, featureless plains and a big open sky. Contains windmills, rectangular voids, and other small features in some areas. The first area discovered by the Zooliminology Project, and usually the initial area you can clip into. Primarily is comprised of vast, open, green planes and rolling knolls that sometimes sport fences. The sky projects the impression of being around midday and usually have large clouds. The grass found here is not confirmed to be related to real-world poaceae. Large black monoliths known as "gateways" can be found scarcely scattered throughout the area.
