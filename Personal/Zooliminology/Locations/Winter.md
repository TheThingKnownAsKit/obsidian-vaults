> [!info] General
> **Size:** Expansive
> **Notable Features:** An exterior part of the Far Plane that is characterized by a constant state of snow and darkness. This area seems to closely resemble a real-world landscape, but the pseudofloran life here does not seem to grow or decay. The sky routinely shifts between being in either sunset or sunrise to being fully night. The area is covered in a constant mist that obscures faraway landmarks. Terrain can range from empty and flat to being covered with pseudoforests. Occasionally dotted with houses.
> 
> **Connects to:** Unknown
> **Habited By:** [[Fogwalkers]], [[Light mimics]]
