There’s several types of gods.
- Patron Gods: These gods are generally the leaders of nations/groups. It is a passed down title. Being the current patron provides resistance to aging (maybe lives 20% longer), disease, and general durability. They are not immortal. Each patron has its own way of passing down the title. 
- Minor Gods: Generally very weak and niche gods. Some of them just live like regular people and you could run into them walking down the street. (favorite best boy god of pastries). Some of them don’t even really have any special abilities besides being more in tune with INSERTMAGICFORCEILLCOMEUPWITHLATERHERE.
- Major Gods: Generally more powerful and broad gods. Usually what people think of as typical gods (Greek, Roman, Norse, etc). 
- Fundamental Gods (come up with a better name later): More forces than actual gods. Can be emotions or fundamental aspects of the world like Life/Afterlife/Hatred/Nature. Most of them don’t manifest in actual forms but do have some sort of conscience that’s barely noticeable.
