# CALCULATING INVENTORY
We will use perpetual
When a sale is made, you need to do two transactions
1. cash or credit? affect appropriate accounts
2. Subtract inventory and increase cost of goods sold expense
Revenue happens at the same time as the expense

In periodic, revenue does Not happen at the same time as the expense

| INVENTORY COST                                               |
| ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| + Merchandise purchase                                       |
| + Shipping and handling costs to get product to your company |
| + Any insurance costs to transport product to your store     |
| + Any costs to store your product until it is sold           |
| - Purchase returns/allowances                                |
| - Purchase discounts                                         |
| = Total Inventory                                            |

*Purchase discounts (discounts given if you pay sooner)*
2/15, net 30
before the / is the percent, so 2%
Pay bill in 15 days
Any remaining balance = net due by 30 days

# INVENTORY ANALYSIS
What do they mean?

Gross profit margin:
* Lower price = more competitive pricing
* OR, cost to make the product increased/decreased

Days inventory outstanding:
* Lower generally means more immediate profit
* Depends on the product (selling cars vs grocery stores)

Days payable outstanding:
* You want higher number because then you have more time to pay off your debt and a better cash conversion number
* Possible sign of trouble paying bills. Need to check if this is within the given supplier timeframe or they're late
