# LIABILITIES
## Current Liabilities
Accounts payable is when you have received the paperwork billing you for the thing and you haven't paid it yet

Accrued liabilities are liabilities incurred but you have not yet received the paperwork charging you. No invoices but you know you owe

**Contractual liabilities** are incurred but not paid

**Contingent liabilities** are past events but the conclusion about future impact is unknown
* Keyword is PAST EVENT. It had to have previously happened within the accounting period

| Possibility                     | Outcome                                                    | Examples                             |
| ------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| Remote                          | No recording or disclosure (unless it's guaranteed credit) | Bad lawsuits you're unlikely to lose |
| Reasonably Possible             | Footnote disclosure                                        | Warranties                           |
| Probable but Can't Be Estimated | Footnote disclosure                                        | Environmental disasters              |
| Probable & Can Be Estimated     | Record a liability in the financial statements             | Penalties from regulators            |
GAAP requires taking the low end of the range of estimations when recording 

## Warranties
* When is the expense recorded?
	* When estimated. Increase a liability and increase an expense (like a Warranty Liability account)
* What do you do when someone brings back the item for repair or replacement?
	* Decrease cash and use up the warranty liability. NOTHING hits the income statement
* When should adjustments be made to warranty liability?
	* T
* What does the balance in warranty liability at the year end represent?
	* Kind of like the allowance for doubtful accounts. It's the amount of money put aside for warranty use. If it's positive then there was leftover money

# STOCKHOLDERS' EQUITY
## Types of Stock
**Preemptive right** sometimes stockholders are given the right to keep the same proportion or % of stock ownership when they issue more shares. So if you have 10% in the company and they issue more shares, they'll give you more shares or give you the opportunity to buy more shares first to keep your 10%

Outstanding = issued - repurchased. Basically homeless shares, ones that are in the market but not bought

Treasury/repurchased stock is a negative equity account

Market capitalization/market cap = stock price x outstanding shares

Book value is total equity / outstanding shares but is largely useless to know because the books aren't updated to current market value of shares regularly so it's stagnant

Preferred stock:
15,000 shares of 100 par value, 8% cumulative preferred stock
* What is dividend? 15000 x 100 x 0.08 = 120,000 fixed amount, no more no less
* Features: Nonvoting, dividend preference, liquidation preference, conversion feature, callable, participating
* Cumulative and noncumulative options
	* **Dividends in arrears** means accrued dividends that have not been paid. Takes priority over common stock. This account is NOT required to be recorded as a liability unless the company declares exactly when it is going to make the payment. Contingent
Preferred stock functions similar to debt except you don't get to use it as as tax deductible so most companies don't use preferred stock

S Company has outstanding 40,000 shares of $50 par value, 6% cumulative preferred  
stock and 100,000 share of $10 par value common stock. The company declares and  
pays cash dividends amounting to $280,000. *If there is 1 year of dividends in arrears*  
*on the preferred stock*, what amount of dividends is paid to each class of stockholder?  
What is the dividends per share per class of stock?

$40,000*50*6\%=120,000$ dividend
Because of arrears, you have to multiply preferred dividends by 2
$120,000*2=240,000$ preferred dividends paid

$280,000-240,000=40,000$ common dividends paid

|                          | Preferred Stockholder | Common Stockholder |
| ------------------------ | --------------------- | ------------------ |
| Dividends Paid           | 240,000               | 40,000             |
| Dividends Paid per Share | $6/share              | $0.4/share         |
Divide dividends by total shares to get dividends paid per share
$240,000/40,000=6$
$40,000/100,000=0.4$
