# CREDIT CARDS
When credit cards are used, the amount is due from the credit card company and NOT the customer
Most credit card companies charge a fee to the company
Most credit card companies deduct the free from the amount they owe to the company

Difference between notes and accounts receivable:
* Payment terms are longer
* Interest is charged - paid at maturity
* Company requires promissory note
* Company requires collateral to ensure they'd get something if you can't pay cash (houses, cars, etc)
