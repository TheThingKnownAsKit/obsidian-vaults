# BUDGETING METHODS
There's four capital budgeting methods:
* Net present value (NPV)
	* Uses the interest rate sheet. If the amount changes every term, use single sum, otherwise use annuity 
* Internal rate of return (IRR)
* Payback period
	* How many years until you get your money back. Literally only about the payback period
* Accounting rate of return
	* Initial investment and avg investment

Time value one's are always better (two first ones) in terms of analysis accuracy
Payback period is really fast and easy
IRR is used for comparing projects of different sizes in terms of benefit % rather than pure number increase

## Cost of Capital
1. What is the average cost a company pays to obtain resources to make investments
2. Effective interest rate on debt
3. Effective dividend rate on preferred stock
4. DPS/MPPS (dividends per share / market price per share) + expected divided growth rate

<mark style="background: #BBFABBA6;">One of these methods is used to determine the cost of capital</mark>
