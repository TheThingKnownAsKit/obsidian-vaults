Things that can be included in the acquisition cost (also known as **asset cost**):
* Sales tax
* Shipping
* Attorney fees or real estate commissions
* Title insurance
* Installation costs
* Remodeling costs for intended purpose

**Depreciable cost** is asset cost - salvage

Note book value is net. The book value of something might be displayed as like Property, Plant, and Equipment net

Double time

1. Determine the straight-line rate (1 divided by number of years)
2. Multiply the straight-line rate by 2
3. Multiply your double rate times the book value at the beginning of the year

Example:
$\frac{1}{4Years}=25\%$
$25*2=50\%$
$.5*18000=9000$

Other

Depreciation and salvage value adjustments will be made to future years and not corrected in past years

Income tax is reduced by depreciation but there are different methods we are not getting into right now

REVENUE EXPENDITURES: <--- THIS IS AN EXPENSE
* *Expense when incurred*
* Ordinary maintenance and repairs
* Lost-cost items (cost so little no one cares; staplers)
* Example: Oil change on car; maintenance on existing air conditioner
CAPITAL EXPENDITURES: <--- THIS IS AN UPDATE TO AN ASSET OR A NEW ASSET (capitalize)
* *Record as an asset when purchased*
* Costs that extend the useful life
* Costs that improve the quality
* Reduce the asset's operating expenses
* Example: new air conditioner for building; replace roof on building
