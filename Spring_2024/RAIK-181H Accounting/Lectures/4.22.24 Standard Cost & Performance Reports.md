# VARIANCE ANALYSIS
Analyzes WHERE something changed so management knows what needs to be changed. There are a few responsibility centers for this,
* Cost Center
	* Only responsible for the expenses
* Revenue Center
	* Only responsible for the revenue
* Profit Center
	* Responsible for revenues and expenses. Things like department stores
* Investment Center
	* Revenues, expenses, and then also things like loans and reinvestments

<mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">Favorable Variance (F)</mark> - Actual results have a favorable outcome on operating income compared to the budget
<mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">Unfavorable Variance (U)</mark> - Actual results have a negative outcome on operating income compared to the budget
Keep in mind what you're actually analyzing. Expenses want to go down, sales want to go up

## Sales Variance

For variance analysis, there are two ways to do it. One is shown below:
![[Pasted image 20240422084901.png]]
Volume variance: BP(change in quantity)
Price variance: AQ(change in price)

<mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">Sales Volume Variance:</mark>
$$
VolumeVariance=BP(AQ - BQ)
$$
	(<mark style="background: #BBFABBA6;">NOTE: negative does NOT necessarily mean unfavorable. If this was an expense it would be favorable. Be careful</mark>)

<mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">Sales Price Variance (Flex budget variance):</mark>
$$
PriceVariance=AQ(AP-BP)
$$

<mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">Net Sales Volume Variance with Contribution Margin</mark> = (AQ - BQ) x Budget Contribution Margin
$$
NetSalesVariance=(AQ-BQ)*BudgetCM
$$
Not using the selling price. This is the PROFIT. 

<mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">Revenue Variance</mark> is the total variance not taking into account costs. It is:
$$
RevenueVariance=(AQ*AP)-(BQ*BP)
$$

BP = Budgeted Price
AP = Actual Price
BQ = Budgeted Quantity
AQ = Actual Quantity

### Revenue Variance Example
#### Sales in Revenues Example
![[Pasted image 20240422085238.png]]
Budgeted quantity = 12,000
Budgeted price = $5.00
Actual quantity = 10,000
Actual price = $5.50

Sales price variance is favorable (f) and sales volume variance is unfavorable (u)

Total static budget = 12,000 x 5 = 60,000
Total actual = 10,000 x 5.5 = 55,000

Overall static budget variance is unfavorable

Total flexible budget = 10,000 x 5.00 = 50,000
Price variance = 55,000 - 50,000 = 5,000 F
Volume variance = 50,000 - 60,000 = -10,000 U (<mark style="background: #BBFABBA6;">NOTE: negative does NOT necessarily mean unfavorable. If this was an expense it would be favorable. Be careful</mark>)

We can quantify how much the change was because of price or volume so we can consult the relevant responsibility center
