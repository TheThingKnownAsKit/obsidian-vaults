## Special Order Examples

* Consider only relevant costs. Hint: Just because it is a variable cost doesn’t automatically make it a relevant cost. 
* Ignore opportunity costs if not operating at capacity. However, if at capacity then consider opportunity costs.
* Don’t forget to consider qualitative factors as well as quantitative results.
* In the long-run, all costs (including fixed) are relevant.
* Full Cost – all costs regardless of activity level or behavior pattern. 
* Consider full cost when agreement is to last multiple years. The full cost per unit should be the minimum acceptable selling price.
