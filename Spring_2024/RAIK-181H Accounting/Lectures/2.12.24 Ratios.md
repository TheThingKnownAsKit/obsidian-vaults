## Financial Ratios
**Common sizing and vertical analysis mean the same thing**. Used for comparing to other companies that are different in size (1million to one company might be peanuts to another)

$VerticalAnalysisOfBalanceSheet=\frac{AccountBalance}{TotalAssets}$

**Trend analysis and horizontal analysis mean the same thing**. Comparing percent change in account balance from base year. Trend analysis over time
$HorizontalAnalysis(TrendAnalysis)=\frac{(CurrentYear-PriorYear)}{PriorYear}$

## Liquidity Ratios
* Focus of liquidity ratios?
	* short-term
* Who is interested in liquidity ratios?
	* short term creditors
		* Bank giving short term loan or line of credit
		* Suppliers
* What does the Current Ratio tell us?
	* do you have enough current assets to cover your current liabilities at this point in time
		* Because your current assets SHOULD be converted into cash this year and your current liabilities are due this year
* What number is a benchmark?
	* 1 or above (special cases are sometimes below a 1)
* What other ratio is similar to the current ratio?
	* Working capital (not on the sheet) = $CurrentAssets - CurrentLiability$. Want it to be positive. Not on the sheet because it is not comparative (need ratio)

* Quick Ratio - How is this different from Current Ratio?
	* Only the three assets to convert to cash first and total liabilities. Theoretically, if you converted your three most liquid assets to cash, could you pay off your debt?
* What is a typical benchmark for this ratio?
	* .5-.75

CASH CONVERSION CYCLE

| Ratio | Other Name | Formula |
| ---- | ---- | ---- |
| Add: Days Inventory Outstanding | Days to sell inventory | 365/InventoryTurnover |
| Add: Days Sales Outstanding | Days to collect receivables | 365/AR Turnover |

| Cash Conversion Cycle | S&P 500 35 days |  |
It takes you 90 days to sell inventory, 30 days to collect from customers, and then if you have 150 days to pay your suppliers, this is
$(90+30)-150=-30$
**Negative is GOOD**. You received cash 30 days before you had to pay anybody. Could be used for other things
**Positive is BAD**. You do not receive cash in time to pay off your debts. They may just need to keep extra cash on hand to cover this, not always a terrible thing
