# FRAUD
Public companies must annually assess and report on the DESIGN and EFFECTIVENESS of internal control over financial reporting
	Design: making the policies
	Effectiveness: following through on the policies in an effective manner (passwords don't mean anything if they're insecure

Cash controls
* Overall
	* Written policies & procedures
	* Segregation of duties
	* Bank reconciliation
* Cash receipts
	* Report immediately when receive
	* Deposit at bank daily
	* Count cash on hand frequently
	* Give customers' receipts
* Cash disbursement
	* Pay with check not cash
	* Look up unused checks
	* Use prenumbered checks
	* Segregation of duties
	* Require supporting documentation for each check
	* Require multiple signatures on large amounts

**Benford's Law**
In any list of financial transactions, the number 1 should occur as the first digit 30.1% of the time, with each successive number occurring as the first digit in lesser percentages, with the number 9 occurring less than 5 percent of the time
	1 is more likely to be the first digit than 9
	Useful for detecting fraud if 9's first digit percentage shoots up somehow
