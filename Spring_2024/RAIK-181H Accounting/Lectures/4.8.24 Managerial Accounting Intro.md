## Assumptions and Terms
Assumptions:
* All costs are classified as variable or fixed
* The total cost and revenue functions are linear within the relevant range
* Only 1 cost driver: unit or dollar sales volume
* Analysis for a single product or sales mix of product is constant

Terminology:
* Costs of making the product:
	* **Direct materials**
	* **Direct labor**
	* **Variable OH (overhead)**
	* **Fixed OH (overhead)**
* Admin costs:
	* **Variable selling & admin**
	* **Fixed selling & admin**

## Break-Even Point and Target Sales
The **break-even point units** = $\frac{FixedCosts}{ContributionMarginPerUnit}$
It is how many units you need to sell to break even. Round up because you can't make half a unit

The **break-even point dollars** = $\frac{FixedCosts}{ContributionMargin\%}$
How many SALES do you need to make to break even? Note the contribution margin % is the unit contribution margin / total sales

However, I like money so let's make some money
**Target sales volume in units** = $\frac{FixedCosts+Profit}{CM/Unit}$
**Target sales volume in dollars** = $\frac{FixedCosts+Profit}{CM\%}$

## Margin of Safety
Measures the cushion between budgeted sales and the break-even point in sales
$$
BudgetedSales-BreakEvenSales=MarginOfSafety
$$
Or you can use sales to get the safety percentage
