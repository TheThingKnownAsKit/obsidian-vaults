
Element: The header categories (assets, liabilities, equity)

A financial statement line item is usually a summary of a lot of sub accounts

**General ledger** is all the transactions that have ever happened since the company started

Cycle (every month):
1. Analyze transactions
	2. Every accounting transaction must BALANCE (assets = liability + equity)
	3. Recorded using double entry accounting - a transaction can't impact only one account
2. Record transactions
	1. 3 types of transactions you can have during a month:
		1. Asset source transaction (asset increase)
		2. Asset use transactions (assets decrease)
		3. Asset exchange transactions (assets dont change)
	2. **Transaction rules:**
		1. Any transaction that impacts cash on the balance sheet will impact the cash flow statement
		2. Any transaction that increases/decreases net income will impact retained earnings
		3. All transactions on the balance sheet much balance (A = L + E)
		4. Dividends will impact retained earnings but not the income statement
3. Adjust accounts
	1. Last day of the month, quarter, or year
	2. What have we used or incurred that has not yet been recorded?
		1. Payroll
	3. What have we earned or sold that has not been recorded?
	4. **Adjusting entries will never impact cash**
4. Prepare financial statements
5. Close temporary accounts

| Permanent | Temporary |
| ---- | ---- |
| Balance sheet<br>Accounts | Income Statement<br>Accounts & Dividends |
| Account balance at the end of the period carries over to the start of the next period | At the end of the year, these accounts are "CLOSED". Account balance is moved to retained earnings |
