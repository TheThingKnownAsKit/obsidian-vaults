	* Cash payments
* Consider how much cash you want on hand
* Consider if you need to borrow any money so you have enough cash on hand
* If you borrow money, factor in interest

# BUDGETING METHODS
* Top-down or imposed budget
	* Top decides and that's it
* Bottom-up or participative budget
	* Everyone participates and gets a say
* What is budgetary slack?
	* Errors in budgeting estimates. Too much budget or too little?
* What is continuous budgeting?
	* Every month a budget is made for sometime in the future. Like in January, you decide on the January for NEXT YEAR
