**Externality** occurs when an economic activity has either a spillover cost to or spillover benefit for a bystander
* Negative impose extra cost
* Positive impose extra benefit

Example: Cigarettes
$Q_{D}=600-6P$
$Q_{S}=30P-30$
$600-6P=30P-30$
$630=36P$
$P=17.5$
$Q=495$
This is the **privately optimal** equilibrium points, where externalities are NOT factored in

**Public goods** are differentiated from private goods in two ways:
* Non-rival: One person's consumption of a non-rival good does not prevent another from consuming it
* Non-excludable: People cannot be prevented from consuming a non-excludable good, even if they don't pay
Prone to issues with **free-riders**: consuming without paying for a good

|                    | Rival                | Non-Rival    |
| ------------------ | -------------------- | ------------ |
| **Excludable**     | Private              | Club goods   |
| **Non-Excludable** | Common pool resource | Public goods |

|                    | Rival                                 | Non-Rival       |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------------- | --------------- |
| **Excludable**     | Most products                         | Internet access |
| **Non-Excludable** | Kauffman laundry<br>Library books<br> | Air             |
