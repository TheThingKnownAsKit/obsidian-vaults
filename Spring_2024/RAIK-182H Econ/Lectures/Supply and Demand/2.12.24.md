If firms leave a market, supply will shift to the left which will increase the marginal revenue
	Market perspective graph vs firm's perspective

*IN A SHORT RUN MARKET SUPPLY CURVE*
If there is profit:
	Firm's will join, increasing supply and decreasing revenue
If there is loss:
	Firm's will leave, decreasing supply and increasing revenue
If there is a net 0:
	No firm's are leaving or joining, stable

*Long run market supply curve is perfectly flat*

The **reservation value** is the price at which you are indifferent between making a trade or not making a trade (willingness to pay/accept)
The difference between your reservation value and the price is your consumer/producer surplus. The sum of the consumer and producer surplus is **social surplus**
	Maximize this through competitive pricing

**Pareto Efficient** means that there is no way to change an allocation/things to make someone better off without making someone else worse
