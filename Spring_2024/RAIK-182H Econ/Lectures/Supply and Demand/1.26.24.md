Limitations of experiments:
* Money
* Ethics
* Happened in the past
* Difficult to apply randomness
* People act differently in labs, Hawthorne Effect
* Experimenter effects: researchers may treat people differently (on purpose or subconscious) that affects the results
	* Lady wonder
* Generalizability: will an intervention conducted on a small group be applicable/effective (does it hold true/does it even work at a large scale) to larger groups at scale

**Natural experiments** are not experiments but are studies where random events happen and researchers divide people into "treatment" and "control" groups
