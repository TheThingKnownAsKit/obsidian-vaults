# LONG RUN ATC CURVES
On a long run ATC curve, the beginning curve is the **economies of scale**, the middle flat part is **constant returns to scale** and the ending curve is **diseconomies of scale**

Most things are the same as short term except there is no such thing as fixed costs. You're not just waking up and dealing with what you have, you can change anything you want

$Profit=(P-ATC)*Q$
Optimal quantity is the intersection of the marginal cost curve and something else

If an economic agent's long run ATC curve is operating at a net loss, they should shut down because fixed costs are not sunk costs on this scale
