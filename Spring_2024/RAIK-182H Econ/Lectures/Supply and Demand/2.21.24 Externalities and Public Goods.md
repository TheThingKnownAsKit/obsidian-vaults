**External costs** implicit costs that most buyers and sellers don't consider
* Using antibiotics has massive benefits but using them also means bacteria gets stronger

A **negative externality** is a cost that is not paid by the seller
* The seller in the above example does not pay anything for antibiotic overuse

**Positive externalities** benefits you do not have to pay for
![[Pasted image 20240221130411.png]]

The general solution to these externalities is to get buyers and sellers to **internalize the externality**. Induce them to account for all costs/benefits

**Coase Theorem** is a private solution. Private bargaining can result in an efficient allocation of resources. The catch is that Coase only applies when **property rights** are clear and **transaction costs** are low
Need:
1. Property rights (cant bargain what you don't own)
2. Transaction costs are low
3. Small number of people/negotiators

Sometimes you need government solutions to externalities. These can include:
* **Command and control regulation**: directly restrict level of production, technology
* **Pigouvian taxes**: tax designed to induce agents who produce negative externalities to reduce quantity to socially optimal level (estimate how much the externalities are, and charge for them)
* **Pigouvian subsidies**: Similar to taxes, but payments to increase quantity
