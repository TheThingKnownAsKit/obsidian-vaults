**Market power** is the influence a firm has over the pricing of their products. If they can afford to price things higher, they have a higher market power
	Concessions at a sporting event

Market power happens when firms sell a unique good and there are **barriers to entry**.

**Legal market power** is when market power is granted to a firm by the government. Usually through patents or copyright

**Natural market power** arises without government intervention. It is acquired naturally or through strategy by the firm
	Can happen through controlling a key resource necessary to produce a certain good
	Goods that exhibit **economics of scale** also tend to lend themselves to market power: Large fixed costs are a barrier to entry

Extreme market power leads to a **monopoly** where only one seller provides a good or service and no decent substitutes exist. Firms with a monopoly can set whatever price they want, they are a **price maker**
	**Residual demand** (demand for the specific firm's good) for its product is perfectly elastic
		In a perfect market it is perfectly flat line because if price is raised even by one cent then consumers will go to a substitute product (D=MR)
		From a monopoly perspective, the residual demand is downward sloping
	They still want to maximize profits
