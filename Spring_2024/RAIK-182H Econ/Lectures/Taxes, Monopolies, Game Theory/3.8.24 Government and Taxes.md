Elasticity and tax incidence impact each other. Remember really elastic supply/demand curves have high variances depending on price
Less elastic curves make quantity fall less and increases tax and decreases deadweight loss
*The moral is that taxes on non elastic goods distort the supply and demand curves less and provide more revenue*

The tax "wedge" is the height of the tax revenue rectangle. Range from the demand curve to the supply curve

Public spending promotes **equity**. However, taxes are a **leaky bucket** where deadweight loss is an **efficiency** cost, Essentially, taxing things to promote services loses efficiency and social surplus. It's a **equity-efficiency tradeoff**

Public policies can be diverted by corruption
Economic activity may be diverted to underground or informal economies (prohibition)
Public policies (the paternalism of government) can violate individual rights
