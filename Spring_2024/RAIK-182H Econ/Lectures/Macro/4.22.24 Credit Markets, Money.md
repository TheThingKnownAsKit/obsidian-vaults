<mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">Crowding out</mark> is when government borrowing displaces firms and households. Essentially when the government increases interest rates so much and buys so much quantity that firms can't compete or afford the interest rates

# BANKS
Banks role in the credit market can be clarified by evaluating their balance sheet
Assets:
* Reserves: Money, federal reserve bank deposits
* Cash equivalents: riskless, liquid assets
* Long-term investments: outstanding loans, real estate
Liabilities:
* Demand deposits: the money in your checking account
* Short-term borrowing: very short borrowing between banks. Loans from other banks
* Long-term debt: have to pay it back greater than a year

<mark style="background: #BBFABBA6;">Banks have three roles:</mark>
1. Identify profitable lending opportunities
2. <mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">Maturity transformation:</mark> turning short-term liabilities into long-term investments. They take money invested into their bank and lend it to other people. This is good for the economy since your money isn't just sitting there and is instead getting recirculated and invested
	1. Maturity is the time until debt must be repaid
3. Management of risk: a bank transfer risk from depositors to bank stockholders and the government. They do this by:
	1. Having a diversified portfolio so if one borrower defaults, the others probable wont. In a severe case a bank becomes <mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">insolvent</mark> when a lot of people default: value of assets < value of liabilities

If everyone tries to withdraw at the same time, this causes a <mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">bank run</mark>. Usually when people fear bank bankruptcy. Banks don't have all your cash on hand, however, so they can't pay everything back. This is why the FDIC insures bank money now

# MONEY
<mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">Money</mark> is defined as any asset used regularly in transactions. It is a,
* Medium of exchange - facilitates trade
* Store of value - you can transfer your purchasing power through time
* Unit of account - universal way to express the value of goods and services

It's a technology/invention
