Essentially, when you only care about finding out if a function converges or diverges, you can simplify the equation into only the important terms and solve.
$\int_2^\infty\frac{x^3}{x^4-1}dx\to\int_2^\infty\frac{x^3}{x^4}dx$ and you can now solve using algebra instead of partial fractions. It's just $\frac{1}{x^4}$. This diverges because of the bounds. Use comparison test to see that f(x) >= g(x) >= 0 so they both diverge
