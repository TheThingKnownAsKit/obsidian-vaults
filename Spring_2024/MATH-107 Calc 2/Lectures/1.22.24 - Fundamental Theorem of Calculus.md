**Fundamental Theorem of Calculus**
For f continuous and F'(x) = f(x),
$\int_a^bf(x)dx=F(b)-F(a)$
and $\frac{d}{dx}\int_a^xf(t)dt=f(x)$

If F'(x) = G'(x), then (F-G)'=0, so F-G is a constant, that is there is a number C such that F(x) = G(x) + c
Two antiderivatives for f(x) differ by c constant. Write the family of all antiderivatives for f. It's just + C
