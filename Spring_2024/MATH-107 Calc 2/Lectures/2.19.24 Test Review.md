# UNFACTORABLE ONE
$\int\frac{4x-3}{x^2+4x+8}dx$

Complete square
$x^2+4x+8$
Take half of b and square it. Add/subtract it to complete the square
$x^2+4x+4-4+8$
$(x^2+4x+4)-4+8$
$\frac{4x-3}{(x+2)^2+4}$

$=\int\frac{4[(x+2)-2]-3}{(x+2)^2+4}dx$
Separate out terms for convenience (complete square in numerator). Distribute 4
$\int\frac{4(x+2)-11}{(x+2)^2+4}dx$

Break into two integrals
$\int\frac{4(x+1)}{(x+2)^2+4}dx-11\int\frac{1}{(x+2)^2+4}dx$
This is A and B

Use u-substitution on integral A
$u=(x+2)^2+4$
$du=2(x+2)dx$
$dx=\frac{du}{2(x+2)}$
$\int\frac{4x+4}{u}\frac{du}{2x+4}$
$\int\frac{2}{u}du$
$=2\ln(|u|)$
$2\ln|(x+2)^2+4|$

Goal: make it look like arctan (easier, but trig subs also work sometimes)
$\frac{1}{(x+2)^2+4}$
Factor out 4
$\frac{1}{4[\frac{(x+2)^2}{4}+1]}$
$\frac{1}{4[(\frac{x+2}{2})^2+1]dx}$
U sub
$u=\frac{x+2}{2}$
$du=1/2dx$
$d=2du$
$\frac{1}{4}\int\frac{1}{u^2+1}2du=\frac{1}{2}\arctan{u}$
$=\frac{1}{2}\arctan(\frac{x+2}{2})$

Add in integral A and B
$\int\frac{4x-3}{x^2+4x+8}dx=2\ln|(x+2)^2+4|+\frac{1}{2}\arctan(\frac{x+2}{2})$
