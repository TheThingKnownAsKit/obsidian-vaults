**INTEGRATION BY PARTS FORMULA**
$\int udv=uv-\int vdu$

Method:
1. Pick u and dv
	1. GUIDELINES FOR CHOOSING U AND DU, pick the one that shows up first:
		1. Inverse Trigonometric or Logarithmic (arcsin, arctan, ln)
		2. Polynomial (exponents are positive integers)
		3. Trigonometric or Exponential (sin, cos, e^x, 7^x)
	2. Generally straightforward integration by parts works for functions that are products of elements in adjacent categories, using u for the lower-numbered (LIKE NUMBER ON THE ABOVE LIST, so 1 is lower than 2) item and dv for the other
2. Find v and dv
	1. Take antiderivative of dv
	2. Take derivative of u
3. Then use the formula

Example:
Find $\int x\cos xdx$
$u=x$
$dv=\cos xdx$
$du=dx$

$\int x\cos xdx=x\sin x-\int\sin xdx$
$=x\sin x-(-\cos x)+C$
$=x\sin x+\cos x+C$

Example:
Find $\int \arctan xdx$
$u=\arctan x$
$dv=dx$
$du=\frac{1}{1+x^2}dx$
$v=x$

$\int \arctan xdx=x\arctan x-\int\frac{x}{1+x^2}dx$
Find the antiderivative of the equation using integration by substitution
$\int\frac{x}{1+x^2}dx$
$w=1+x^2$
$dw=2xdx$
$\int \frac{1}{w} \frac{dw}{2}=\frac{1}{2}\int\frac{dw}{w}$
$=\frac{1}{2}\ln|w|$
Substitute w back in
$\frac{1}{2}\ln|1+x^2|$

BACK INTO THE MAIN SUBSTITUTION
$\int \arctan xdx=x\arctan(x)-\frac{1}{2}\ln(1+x^2)+C$

Example:
Find $\int xe^xdx$
$u=x$
$dv=e^xdx$
$du=dx$
$v=e^x$

$\int xe^xdx=xe^{x}-{\int} e^xdx$
$=xe^x-e^x+C$
