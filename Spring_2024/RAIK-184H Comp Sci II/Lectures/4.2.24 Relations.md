A **binary relation** is an ordered pair. A binary relation's **inverse** generates the same pairs in the opposite order
	In a graph you reverse the direction of the arrow
	In a sentence you switch the variables
	In an equation solve for the other variable

When you **compose** functions, you perform a search using one relation and then perform the next search using the result of the previous search. Think of it like maps
	If you have major x student composed inside student x room. First, you search by major, then you take that student and find their room. It becomes major x room (notice student gets dropped, middle man gets cut out)
