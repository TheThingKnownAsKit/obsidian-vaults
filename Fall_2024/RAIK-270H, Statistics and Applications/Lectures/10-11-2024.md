## Poisson Process
Generally, Poisson models arrival times. Frequently used to model insurance claims, traffic flow, and inventory systems
Whole point is that it's a skewed distribution

Poisson distribution models a <mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">Poisson process</mark> which is counting the number of events that have occurred between time 0 and t
Note: Events are disjointed (non-overlapping) and independent
Key variables are:
* $\mu=\lambda$
* $\sigma^2=\lambda$
* $\sigma=\sqrt\lambda$
