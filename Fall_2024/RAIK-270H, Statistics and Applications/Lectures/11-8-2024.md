## Welch's T-Test
<mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">Welch's t-test</mark> gives the probability that two sets of data come from the SAME original population
	Null hypothesis: the two groups of data come from the same group
<mark style="background: #BBFABBA6;">The key difference is that Welch's t-test does NOT assume equal variances</mark>. Variance is not pooled. Dealing with MULTIPLE distributions

In Excel/Sheets, use two sample assuming unequal variances (TTEST(array1, array2, 3, 2))
In Python, use scipy.stats.ttest_ind and pass in the argument equal_var=False

## Pre- and Post-testing
Basically just use a control group where your experiment condition is not applied to see if your experiment condition is even doing anything

If you want to know how a group changed based off your experiment condition you have to use <mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">paired t-testing</mark> instead of Student's or Welch's
