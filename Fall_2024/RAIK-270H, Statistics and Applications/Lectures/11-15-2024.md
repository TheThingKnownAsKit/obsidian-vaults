## ANOVA
![[Pasted image 20241115161018.png]]
We will just focus on one-way variables

Student's t-test compares the means, while <mark style="background: #BBFABBA6;">ANOVA compares the VARIANCES between populations</mark>. It will give you a single number (the f-statistic) and one p-value to help you support or reject the null hypothesis

The <mark style="background: #BBFABBA6;">null hypothesis is that there is no difference in the means across the groups</mark>. The hypothesis is that each group has its own mean.

STEP 1
![[Pasted image 20241115162536.png]]
![[Pasted image 20241115162545.png]]

For a single variable across multiple groups (one-way, most common), use [scipy.stats.f_oneway](https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy/reference/generated/scipy.stats.f_oneway.html)
For two variables across multiple groups (two-way, will not need in this course), you’ll need another library like [statsmodels.stats.anova.anova_lm](https://www.statsmodels.org/stable/generated/statsmodels.stats.anova.anova_lm.html)
