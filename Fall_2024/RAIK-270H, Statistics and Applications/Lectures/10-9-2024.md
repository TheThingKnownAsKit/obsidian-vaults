## Binomial Distribution
Binomial distributions are essentially discrete bell curves for probabilities. Assumes independence

Bernoulli distribution is when there's only one event can happen

Hypergeometric distributions are a special case where the events are NOT independent of each other

The Poisson distribution is the probability of k independent, randomly-occurring events happening over time or space where $\lambda$ events happen on average
$$P(k)=e^{-\lambda}\frac{\lambda^2}{k!}$$
