<mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">Data</mark> are facts and statistics collected together for reference or analysis
Types of data include facts and statistics
Statistics is referencing and analysis

Data comes in two primary forms:
1. <mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">Quantitative</mark> - MEASURABLE data. It can be quantified. Usually numbers and orderable. Good with computers and is objective. Constrains types of data, not everything can be a number
2. <mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">Qualitative</mark> - Relating to quality not quantity. Gives rich details that are hard to capture otherwise. Can be subjective or involves more extensive coding work. Usually text not numbers
	1. Sometimes referred to as categorical data because it can be confusing to have two words so close together
<mark style="background: #BBFABBA6;">It is more about numbers vs not numbers than subject vs non subjective</mark>. Objective facts can still be qualitative if it is represented in text or such. The FORMS of values in your dataset. Nature is objective and subjective
