## RISC (ARM)
Philosophy: Make it simple so it can be made fast

* MIPS processor - one million instructions per second
* RISC is simple architecture:
	* No condition codes
	* Only direct & offset addressing modes
	* No move instruction, compiler uses add

Small, fast, power-efficient processing
